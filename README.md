## Featured projects

**[Airline departure punctuality](https://github.com/TheLordBass/Airline-Departure-Punctuality-Analysis)** · Power BI, Power Query, DAX  
18 months of short-haul departures. On-time performance averaged 78.2% against an 80% target, and knock-on delays from late inbound aircraft cost the most minutes per event, which points at turnaround buffer as the highest-leverage fix.

**[US electric vehicle adoption](https://github.com/TheLordBass/Analyzing-U.S.-Electric-Vehicle-Market-Share)** · SQL Server, Power BI  
Vehicle registrations across all 50 states and DC. California holds 35% of America's EVs, while Texas has almost as many EVs as Florida but the lowest adoption rate of the four largest states, because its fleet is so big.

**[Mobile game revenue](https://github.com/TheLordBass/Mobile-Game-Revenue-Analysis)** · Power BI, Power Query, DAX  
18 months of in-app purchases. The top 10% of paying players generate 64.5% of revenue, and growth came from more people paying, not from people paying more.

**[Airline customer complaints](https://github.com/TheLordBass/Airline-Customer-Complaints-Analysis)** · Power BI  
977 complaints over 18 months. Complaint volume barely moves with punctuality (correlation 0.11). The problem worth fixing is a uniform 22-day resolution time.

**[Premier League season dashboard](https://github.com/TheLordBass/Premier-League-Analysis-with-Excel-)** · Excel, Power Query, dynamic arrays  
Rebuilds the league table for any season from 2000/01 to 2021/22 out of 8,360 match results, and writes its own season summary. The empty-stadium 2020/21 season was the only one in 22 where away teams won more often than home teams.

**[Uber driver bonus schemes](https://github.com/TheLordBass/Partner-Business-Modeling)** · SQL  
Two incentive schemes for a surge Saturday. The flat-rate option cost $1,050 against $2,696 for pay-per-trip, under 40% of the price.

**[NBA trends, 1996–2023](https://github.com/TheLordBass/NBA-Player-Stats)** · SQL (CTEs, window functions)  
Over 12,000 player seasons, measuring the rise of "small ball" and testing whether high-usage scorers really are less efficient. They aren't.

## How I work

- **Rates as well as counts.** Big states and big airports always win on volume, so I normalise (EVs per registered vehicle, complaints per 1,000 flights) to make the comparison fair.
- **Cleaning decisions, written down.** Not just "removed nulls" but why: sentinel values nulled rather than filtered so real flights aren't lost, and test accounts removed at load so every visual is right by default.
- **Honest about limits.** Hypotheses are labelled as hypotheses, and data-quality problems go up front, like the 11% of airline delays with no cause recorded.
- **Numbers checked before they're shared.** Deduplication caught duplicate transactions overstating game revenue by 1.3%. Small enough to pass unnoticed, which is exactly why I check.
